Key Parameter TDS

Total Dissolved Solids

BIS Acceptable Limit: 500 mg/L (Permissible: 2000 mg/L). High TDS affects taste and hardness; not automatically toxic.

Key Parameter pH

Acidity / Alkalinity

BIS Desirable Range: 6.5 to 8.5. Low pH corrodes metal pipes; high pH creates scale and reduces disinfection efficiency.

Key Parameter Turbidity

Water Clarity (NTU)

BIS Standard: < 1 NTU (Permissible: 5 NTU). Cloudiness shelters microbes from chlorine disinfection.

📚 Water Parameter Knowledge Center

Scientific benchmarks, physical meanings, and public health impact according to BIS IS 10500:2012

TDS Unit: mg/L (ppm)

Total Dissolved Solids

The combined total of all organic and inorganic substances dissolved in water, predominantly calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, chlorides, and sulfates.

Acceptable Limit: Up to 500 mg/L
Permissible Limit: 2000 mg/L (if no alt source)
pH Logarithmic Scale

Potential of Hydrogen

Measures how acidic or basic water is on a scale from 0 to 14. Neutral is 7.0. Water with pH < 6.5 can corrode plumbing; pH > 8.5 imparts a slippery feel and soda taste.

Desirable Range: 6.5 to 8.5
Extreme Warning: <6.0 or >9.2
TURBIDITY Unit: NTU

Cloudiness & Suspended Matter

The haziness of water caused by large numbers of individual particles (silt, clay, pathogens). High turbidity shields microbes from chlorine disinfection.

Acceptable Limit: < 1.0 NTU
Permissible Limit: 5.0 NTU
HARDNESS as CaCO3 mg/L

Total Hardness

Primarily caused by dissolved calcium and magnesium ions. Hard water prevents soap lathering and creates limescale inside geysers, pipes, and cooking pots.

Acceptable Limit: 200 mg/L
Permissible Limit: 600 mg/L
EC Unit: µS/cm

Electrical Conductivity

Measures water's ability to conduct an electric charge. Highly correlated with TDS because dissolved mineral salts dissociate into conductive ions.

Normal Range: 100 - 800 µS/cm
Saline / High Ion: > 1500 µS/cm
FACT CHECK Public Health

Does Boiling Remove TDS?

NO. Boiling kills biological bacteria and pathogens, but evaporates pure water vapour, slightly increasing the concentration of dissolved minerals!

For biological safety: Boil / UV.
For high TDS / chemical salts: Reverse Osmosis.

About Project JalCheck

JalCheck is a Community Engagement Project (CEP) combining environmental science, data science, geographical visualization, and educational HCI (Human-Computer Interaction).

1. Research Objectives

Identify water quality disparities across municipal and groundwater sources; educate community residents without inciting panic; evaluate public water literacy through structured surveys.

2. Scientific Methodology

Calibrated field probes and standard laboratory titrations (APHA standard methods) evaluated against Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS IS 10500:2012).

3. Professor Aqua USP

A universal, context-aware pedagogical guide with multi-state animations and voice synthesis that demystifies scientific graphs for citizens of any educational background.

Architecture: Pure Vanilla JS (ES6+) + Tailwind CSS + FastAPI + SQLite + Pandas/NumPy
Project Evaluator Guide: Full CRUD endpoints available for administrator CSV ingest and survey export.
